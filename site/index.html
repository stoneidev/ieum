Questions, answered plainly.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Recognition and translation run on your Mac. The first time you use a language, macOS downloads its model, and that single step needs a connection.

Can the other people in the meeting see Ieum?

No. The captions are a window on your own screen. Nothing is added to your video call, and the call software does not know it is there.

Can it caption a video call, not just a microphone?

Yes. Choose system audio and Ieum captions what the Mac is playing. macOS will ask for Screen & System Audio Recording permission the first time.

Which languages does it support?

English (US and Australian) and Korean, in both directions. The command-line version can transcribe more languages that macOS supports on-device.

What do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac. Captions work on macOS 14 or later. The faster recognizer, conversation mode and translation need macOS 26.4 or later.

Will it get my company's names right?

Add them to the vocabulary list before the meeting. Ieum corrects them in the transcript before translating. Apple's recognizer does not take hints directly, so this correction is done afterwards.

What does Ieum mean?

이음 means "to connect". A join between two sides that were apart.
